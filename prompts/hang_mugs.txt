Control the dual ARX X5 robot for hang_mugs. There are three mugs and one mug rack. The robot needs to pick up each mug and hang all of them on the rack.

Completion requirement: All three mugs are hung on the rack and the robot returns to origin.

Use only current RGB, camera calibration and measured robot state. Historical demonstrations are examples. Adapt their object count, positions, dimensions and order to the current scene.

Persistent python_cell can retain measurements, geometry functions and short motion helpers. Keep the cup handle free when choosing a grasp on the rim or body. Choose a visible peg and align the handle opening with its axis using current images; the TCP and handle center are different points. Optional measure_mug_geometry/robo.measure estimates matched points, peg endpoints/radius or a planar inner opening from RGB. Select the same physical points in both views, including a useful working-wrist view; poor visibility is not evidence of engagement.

For a held mug, robo.move_feature can rotate about a measured handle center or translate it toward a peg while accounting for the TCP offset. This assumes a rigid grasp. After contact or slip, use current RGB and measured robot pose to revise the feature position and next short action. Closing and settling with robo.grip/robo.hold hold measured joints; they do not certify a grasp. Once the handle is supported, release and withdraw clear, then inspect suspension before moving to another mug. Choose the grasp, order, motion, recovery and completion yourself; tools do not execute a hanging program.
